# PetLog

A simple pet health diary built with Python and Streamlit. Log daily notes about your pets'
appetite, sleep, exercise and general wellbeing, and keep track of how they are
doing over time.



## Planned features


- **User accounts:** register and log in to keep your pets' data private and saved
- **Multiple pets:** track several pets at once, each with their own profile and species
- **Daily entries:** record appetite, sleep, exercise and mood, plus extra fields specific to each species
- **Health events:** track illnesses, medication, vaccinations and vet visits
- **History and charts:** see how your pet's wellbeing changes over time
- **Deleting account:** export your data as CSV and delete your account at any time
